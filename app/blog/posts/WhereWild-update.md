# What's up with WhereWild?

I've been busy all summer with adding a lot of stuff to WhereWild! I was rushing to try and get it in a polished, stable state before school starts up again, as I likely won't have very much time to work on it for the forseeable future. I'm very happy with where it's currently at right now. There's so much that was added, and much more than there is space to discuss here. You can view the tutorial at [wherewild.net/about](wherewild.net/about).

In general, the "base" features are mostly complete. I plan to still develop the app, but at a slower, more deliberate pace, documenting more of my approach as I go. Some of the features I plan to add in the future are:

- A more complete "species" page for taxa at a higher level than species, such as the family level, with a functioning map, filters, etc.
- Species distribution models with an interpretability focus (e.g. SHAP, LIME) to explain why species are and aren't present in a given location
- Native plant recommendations for a garden, utilizing interpretable SDMs and input such as slope or shade to calculate suitability
- More environmental variables, including very-fine grained ones with focus on modelling microclimates, such as water runoff derived from DEM, etc

And probably much, much more!

Planning on starting to "market" the project around soon since almost no one knows about it, so hopefully it can get more eyes on it soon.
